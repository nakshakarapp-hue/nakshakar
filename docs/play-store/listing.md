## Full description (4000 characters max)

Nakshakar is a house planning app made for India: for house owners, mestris, builders, contractors and engineers.

PLAN ROOM BY ROOM
• Pick a room and a size, and tap Add. Walls, a door, a window and furniture go in by themselves.
• Draw walls corner after corner, or type exact lengths in feet and inches, metres or gaj.
• Pillars, beams, slabs, stairs of every shape, doors, windows, furniture and plants, chosen by picture.
• Change anything after adding it: tap it, type a new size or drag its corners.
• Several floors, each with its own undo.

SEE IT AND WALK THROUGH IT
• Turn the house in 3D and look inside floor by floor.
• Walk up to the front door, open doors and windows, climb the stairs.
• Paint walls, floors, roofs and furniture in any colour.

KNOW THE COST
• Cost, materials and days as a range: minimum, most likely and maximum.
• Cement, steel, bricks, sand and aggregate quantities.
• Type your own local rates and the estimate updates at once.

CHECK AND PRINT
• Vastu check with reasons you can read or exclude.
• Printed plans with dimensions, north arrow and scale, as a PDF.

SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY OR BUILDER
• Send a house as one file on WhatsApp or email. They can look through it or keep a copy.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
• Works without internet. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your houses stay on your phone.
• English, Hindi and Telugu, with common words in six more Indian languages.

Nakshakar is a planning tool. A licensed engineer must design the structure, and estimates are planning figures to compare with local quotes.
