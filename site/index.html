Everything a house plan needs

For house owners, mestris, builders, contractors and engineers.

  • Rooms to size

    Room after room with shared walls, and a preview of where the next one goes.

  • Real units

    Feet and inches, metres or gaj. Trace your plot with setbacks, or type every wall.

  • Floors

    Storeys, a basement or a terrace, each with its own undo, in any order.

  • 3D walkthrough

    Start at the front door, open doors and windows, and go from room to room.

  • Finishes

    Doors, windows, furniture and plants by picture. Paint walls, floors and roofs.

  • Vastu check

    A score with reasons you can read, and rules you can leave out.

  • Estimates as ranges

    Cement, steel, bricks and labour, with every assumption listed.

  • Printed plans

    Vector PDF plans with dimensions, north arrow and scale.

  • Share a house

    Send one file on WhatsApp. They can look through it or keep a copy.

Stand inside your house before it is built

Downloads

The same app on every device. Choose yours, or use it straight away in a browser with Open site.

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Runs in its own window, with no internet needed.

  2. If Windows shows an "unknown publisher" warning, choose More info, then Run anyway. The app is not code-signed.

Android

Phones and tablets. It asks for no internet permission at all.

iPhone and iPad
