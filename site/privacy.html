Nakshakar

Privacy policy

Applies to the Nakshakar website, the Android app and the Windows app. Last updated 6 November 2026.

In short

Nakshakar does not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. Your houses stay on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your projects, saved versions, prices you type and settings are stored only on the device you use, in the app's own storage.
  • Nothing you draw or type is sent to us or to anyone else.

The one thing the app fetches

When the app opens, it reads one small public file from this website that says what the newest version of Nakshakar is, so it can tell you when an update is ready. Nothing about you, your device or your houses is sent with that request. Like any website, the server that hosts the file may keep standard technical logs, such as the address the request came from, for a short time; we do not receive or use them.

Sharing a house

When you choose Share, the app makes a file of that house and hands it to your phone's or computer's own sharing options. Where it goes is up to you. The file contains the whole design, so send it only to people you would show the drawings to.

Permissions on Android

  • Internet: only to read the new-version file described above.
  • Install apps (only in the copy downloaded from this website, not the Google Play copy): to install an update you have chosen to download. Android asks you before allowing it.
  • Files you open or share are accessed only when you pick them.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
